FAQ

Questions, answered.

Most common questions we get from shop operators, students and home users. More in our contact page.

Is Studio Print really free?

Yes — the free tier gives you 2 A4 sheets per day with Indian passport presets, no signup wall, and 300 DPI print-ready output. Upgrade to Weekly (₹59) or Monthly (₹149) only if you need unlimited sheets or all country presets.

Does my photo get uploaded to a server?

No. Studio Print runs entirely inside your browser. AI background removal, cropping, and A4 sheet rendering all happen on your device. Customer photos and documents never leave the browser.

Which passport photo sizes and countries are supported?

India (3.5×4.5 cm and 5×5 cm), US Visa (2×2 inch), UK, Schengen, UAE, Australia, Canada and more. All country presets are unlocked on the paid plan; the free tier includes the Indian passport preset.

Do I need Photoshop or any other software?

No. Everything runs in a normal web browser on a laptop, phone or tablet. You don't need Photoshop, Illustrator, or any desktop app — just upload a photo and download the print-ready sheet.

Can it print Aadhaar or PAN card sheets?

The dedicated Aadhaar / PAN "Document Sheet" tool is in the works and will land soon. For now, you can already use Passport Photo for ID-sized photo sheets. Follow the homepage for updates.

Does Studio Print work on mobile?

Yes. The tool is designed mobile-first and works on any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox — on Android, iPhone, iPad, or a desktop. 4G connection and 2 GB RAM are enough.

How do refunds work?

Both Weekly and Monthly plans are one-time purchases (no auto-renewal). Duplicate charges and failed activations are refunded in full within 5-7 working days. Full details in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

I run a print shop or kiosk — can I use this at my counter?

Yes, that is exactly what Studio Print was built for. The Monthly ₹149 plan is designed for counters that process many sheets a day. No install, no Photoshop licence — just open the browser on your shop PC or tablet.
