FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out via the contact page.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?
No. The AI background removal and all cropping happen entirely inside your browser using transformers.js. Your image never leaves your device.
Will the output be accepted for my passport / visa?
Studio Print uses the official dimensions published by each country for passport and visa photos. The 300-DPI A4 output is designed to meet print-shop standards. Final acceptance always depends on the issuing authority, so double-check your local requirements before printing in bulk.
Do I need to create an account?
No account is required to use the free tier. Sign up only when you want to upgrade to Weekly or Monthly for unlimited sheets — Google Sign-In and email signup are both supported.
Which countries are supported?
India, US (passport + visa), UK, UAE, Schengen, Australia, Canada, Singapore and more — 20+ presets built in, with exact dimensions for each.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. Weekly and Monthly plans are one-time payments with a fixed duration — nothing auto-renews. You can stop any time by simply not paying again.
