- `https://pehchan.rajasthan.gov.in/pehchan3/docs/120112023121043.pdf`: not listed directly because pages 1-5 are notification/circular material; Form 15 was taken from the cleaner current 2025 rules source instead.
- `https://pension.rajasthan.gov.in/img/download/Family%20Pension%20Forms.pdf`: rejected because it is a scan bundle with existing pen dates, signatures, stamps, dark/tilted pages, and official letters mixed with forms.
- `https://pension.rajasthan.gov.in/img/pdf/Notice.pdf`: rejected because it is a signed/scanned notice/letter pack, not a clean blank printable form.
- `https://pension.rajasthan.gov.in/img/pdf/VariousOtherForms.pdf`: rejected as a mixed scanned bundle with dirty/tilted/stamped pages and multiple unrelated workflows; not safe to list as one clean form.
- `pre-88.pdf` / Form Pre88 candidate: official table label was found, but a clean existing direct PDF URL was not verified in this pass.
- Pension revision candidate: official table label was found, but the available indexed candidates were circulars/memos, not clean blank application forms.
