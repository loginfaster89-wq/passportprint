About Studio Print

A fast, private, browser-based print studio — built for Indian print shops, students and home users.

Studio Print is a browser-based multi-tool studio for the prints a small shop or home user actually runs — passport photos, Aadhaar and PAN card sheets, certificate edits, A4 layouts and more. Upload a photo or document, let our on-device AI clean it up, and download a 300-DPI A4 sheet with cutting guides. Take it to any ₹10 print counter and you’re done.

The first tool we shipped is Passport Photo — turn a selfie into a government-compliant passport photo with country presets for India, US, UK, UAE, Schengen and more. Document Sheet, Certificate Editor, ID & Visa Sheets and Background Studio are rolling out next.

Why we built it

The idea came from watching a shop operator spend fifteen minutes in Photoshop for a single ₹10 passport print. Most passport-photo websites either charge too much, require installing a desktop app, or upload your photo to a remote server. We wanted something different:

  • Completely private. Your photo never leaves your browser. All AI and cropping happens on-device.
  • Print-shop ready. 300 DPI A4 output with thin cutting lines, so any local printer can cut it cleanly.
  • Accurate. Built-in presets for common passport sizes — India, US Visa, UAE, Schengen, Australia and more.
  • Affordable. Free forever (with a 2-sheet-per-day limit). Full access — unlimited sheets, HD export and all country presets — is ₹59 for a week or ₹149 for a month.

How it works

  1. Upload a clear selfie or studio photo.
  2. Remove the background with AI (the briaai/RMBG-1.4 model, running in your browser via transformers.js).
  3. Crop & adjust to the preset of your choice.
  4. Generate a 300-DPI A4 sheet and download.
