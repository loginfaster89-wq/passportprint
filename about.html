Our mission

Make every ₹10 print job feel like a browser tab.

Studio Print is a browser-based print studio for the jobs a small shop or home user actually runs — passport photos, Aadhaar and PAN card sheets, certificate edits, 300 DPI A4 layouts. Every AI step runs on your own device. Nothing uploads.

Why we built it

Most passport-photo tools charge too much, need a desktop install, or upload your photo to a remote server. We wanted a fourth option — a browser tab that just works, privately, on any laptop or phone.

“Fifteen minutes in Photoshop for a single ₹10 passport print — that’s what we watched a shop operator do. Studio Print exists so that takes ten seconds instead.” — Studio Print team
  • Completely private. Your photo never leaves your browser. All AI and cropping happens on-device.
  • Print-shop ready. 300 DPI A4 output with thin cutting lines, so any local printer can cut it cleanly.
  • Accurate. Built-in presets for common passport sizes — India, US Visa, UAE, Schengen, Australia and more.
  • Affordable. Free forever (with a 2-sheet-per-day limit). Full access — unlimited sheets, HD export and all country presets — is ₹59 for a week or ₹149 for a month.

What ships next

Coming in 2026 The rest of the studio

Passport Photo is live. These tools are already in internal testing and ship one by one through 2026:

  • Document SheetAadhaar, PAN and other IDs arranged on a single A4 with cut lines.
  • Certificate EditorMarriage, birth and misc. certificate edits — no desktop tool needed.
  • ID & Visa SheetsMix passport, visa and ID sizes on one sheet to save paper.
  • Background StudioRemove or replace backgrounds for product and profile photos.

How it works

  1. Upload a clear selfie or studio photo.
  2. Remove the background with AI (the briaai/RMBG-1.4 model, running in your browser via transformers.js).
  3. Crop & adjust to the preset of your choice.
  4. Generate a 300 DPI A4 sheet and download.
