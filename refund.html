Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 20 April 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel your Studio Print subscription and when you are eligible for a refund. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. Cancellation

  • Our Weekly (₹59 / 7 days) and Monthly (₹149 / 30 days) plans are one-time purchases. They do not auto-renew.
  • You do not need to cancel anything to stop being billed — no further charge will happen unless you manually buy a new plan.
  • You may stop using the Service at any time and delete your account from My Account → Delete Account Permanently.

2. Refund eligibility

We want you to be happy with the Service. You may request a full refund in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment — if Razorpay has charged you twice for the same plan, we will refund the duplicate charge in full.
  • Plan not activated — if your payment was successful but your plan was not activated on your account within 24 hours, we will refund the charge.
  • Service unavailability — if our backend is down for a continuous period of more than 48 hours during your paid plan, you can request a pro-rata refund for the unusable days.

3. Refunds not available

Because Studio Print is a digital product that is delivered and usable immediately after purchase, refunds are not available in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after the plan is activated and has already been used.
  • You did not read the feature list, daily limits or plan duration before purchasing.
  • The photos you produced were rejected by a third-party passport / visa authority. (Acceptance is always subject to the issuer’s own rules — please verify dimensions, background and composition before printing.)
  • You no longer need the Service before the plan period ends.

4. How to request a refund

Email us at loginfaster89@gmail.com within 7 days of the original payment with:

  1. The Gmail address associated with your Studio Print account.
  2. The Razorpay order_id or payment_id from the transaction receipt (Razorpay sends you one by email).
  3. A short description of why you are requesting a refund.

We will review the request and respond within 3 working days.

5. Refund processing time

  • Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method through Razorpay.
  • Once issued, the amount typically appears in your bank / card / UPI account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Studio Print cannot influence the exact settlement time once the refund has been initiated through Razorpay.

6. Chargebacks

If you disagree with a charge please contact us first. Filing a chargeback with your bank without contacting us may result in your Studio Print account being permanently suspended.
